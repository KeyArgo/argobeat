# Deep Work Profile

**BPM:** 80-100
**Sound:** Dark, warm, subdued, low event density
**Rhythm:** Very minimal

## Brain.fm Science

- Amplitude modulation target: 17.4 Hz (high beta)
- Research: brain.fm learn ref ~6 Hz, ~90.7 BPM
- Deep Work is more distinct from Focus because it uses lower modulation frequency
- Positioned as "Flow/Learn" in research — supports sustained concentration without stimulation

## Direction

Deep Work sits between Focus and Relax. It's more subdued than Focus but still has enough presence to support sustained concentration. Good for reading, writing, or long coding sessions where Focus might be too stimulating.

## Instruments

- Analog synth pad, slowly evolving
- Felt piano, soft sustained chords
- Sub bass drone, deep bass

DO NOT use:
- Electric guitar
- Orchestral strings (too emotional for deep work)
- Any bright or harsh sounds

## Post-Processing

- Lowpass: 1400Hz
- Target LUFS: -18
- Compression to even out dynamics
- Brickwall limit to prevent clipping

## What This Is NOT

- Not as energetic as Focus
- Not nature-first (that's Relax)
- Not near-static (that's Sleep)
