# Sleep Profile

**BPM:** 20-40
**Sound:** Almost nothing happening, soft hum, no surprises
**Rhythm:** None, near-static

## Brain.fm Science

- Amplitude modulation target: 2.0 Hz (delta)
- Research: delta waves (0.8-2 Hz) associated with deep sleep
- Noise as sleep aid systematic review (ScienceDirect) supports brown/pink noise
- brain.fm sleep music article: lowest possible salience, no events

## Direction

Sleep is the strictest category. Almost nothing happens. Very light brown/pink-ish noise or soft hum. No surprises, no busy nature detail, no foreground instruments. Safe to leave on while falling asleep.

## Instruments

- Very light brown noise, soft hum, minimal
- Sub bass drone, deep bass, barely audible
- Analog synth pad, warm analog, nearly silent

## Post-Processing

- Lowpass: 800Hz (aggressive — remove all highs)
- Target LUFS: -22 (quiet)
- Fade in: 15s, Fade out: 20s

## Rules

- No obvious events
- No foreground instruments
- No big stereo movement
- No melody
- No gongs, birds, bright rain
- No rhythmic changes
- Should be safe to sleep with on

## What This Is NOT

- Not stimulating enough for Relax
- Not trance-like (that's Meditate)
- Not rhythmic (that's Focus)
- Not nature-rich (that's Relax)
