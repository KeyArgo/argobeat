# Relax Profile

**BPM:** 50-80
**Sound:** Calm, pleasant, serene nature-first, subdued drone
**Rhythm:** None

## Brain.fm Science

- Amplitude modulation target: 10.7 Hz (alpha)
- Research: alpha waves promote calm alertness and relaxation
- Nature sounds should be gentle and predictable, never dramatic
- Low salience is key — nothing should grab attention

## Direction

Relax is serene nature calm. Gentle rain, soft stream, distant ocean — only calm and peaceful nature. Layered with a subdued drone. Not trance-like, not task-specific — just pleasant background calm.

## Instruments

- Analog synth pad, warm analog, slowly evolving
- Felt piano, soft sustained chords
- Orchestral strings, sustained bowing, barely audible

## Nature Layers (CALM ONLY)

- Gentle rain (steady, not downpour)
- Soft stream / river (trickling, not rushing)
- Distant ocean (gentle lapping, not crashing waves)
- Light birds (occasional, not busy flock)
- Soft fire crackle (optional, gentle)

DO NOT use:
- Storms, thunder, lightning
- Wind howling or gusting
- Crashing waves, surf
- Heavy rain, downpour, deluge
- Any chaotic or dramatic nature sounds

Layer 1-3 nature sounds at -8 to -15 dB
Optional drone at -22 dB

## Post-Processing

- Lowpass: 1200Hz
- Target LUFS: -17
- Fade in: 5s, Fade out: 8s

## What This Is NOT

- Not task-specific (that's Focus or Deep Work)
- Not trance-like (that's Meditate)
- Not near-static (that's Sleep)
- Not rhythm-driven (that's Focus)
- Not stormy or dramatic
