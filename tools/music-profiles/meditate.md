# Meditate Profile

**BPM:** 30-50
**Sound:** Trance-like, sparse gongs/bowls, long decay, ritual calm
**Rhythm:** None, very sparse events

## Brain.fm Science

- Amplitude modulation target: 5.3 Hz (theta)
- Research: theta waves associated with deep meditation and trance states
- Singing bowl study (Sage Journals) supports bowl resonance for meditation
- Very low event density prevents cognitive load

## Direction

Meditate is intentional and trance-like. Sparse gongs, singing bowls, long decay. Very low event density — not a chime recital, not busy nature. The goal is calm entrainment with minimal sensory load. Think Buddhist temple, not spa music.

## Instruments

- Singing bowls, gongs, long decay, sparse
- Gongs, bells, mallets, textural, very slow
- Analog synth pad, warm analog, minimal

## Post-Processing

- Lowpass: 1000Hz
- Target LUFS: -20
- Fade in: 8s, Fade out: 12s

## Gong/Bowl Pattern

- Gongs at 5+ minute intervals
- Long decay (10-30 seconds per strike)
- No rhythmic pattern — irregular, ritual-like

## What This Is NOT

- Not nature-first (that's Relax)
- Not busy or chime-heavy
- Not near-static (that's Sleep)
- Not rhythm-driven (that's Focus)
