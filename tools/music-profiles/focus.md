# Focus Profile

**BPM:** 100-130
**Sound:** Dark, warm, motivating, low harshness
**Rhythm:** Minimal, not zero — enough to feel like music

## Brain.fm Science

- Amplitude modulation target: 15.7 Hz (beta entrainment)
- Research: brain.fm neural phase-locking — direct modulation in audio channels is stronger than binaural beats
- Focus tracks should have rhythmic elements that complement 15.7 Hz modulation, not fight it
- Multi-band approach: brain.fm targets both beta AND gamma for focus

## Direction

Focus tracks should feel like productive background music. Not static drone, but not busy either. The rhythm is minimal but present — enough motion to keep you engaged without distraction.

## Instruments

- Warm muted electric piano, soft filtered synth pulse
- Analog synth pad, warm analog
- Felt piano with soft sustained chords
- Clean low arpeggio, muted synth keys

DO NOT use:
- Electric guitar (even swells)
- Orchestral strings (too emotional/distracting)
- Any bright or harsh lead sounds

## Post-Processing

- Lowpass: 1500Hz
- Target LUFS: -16
- Compression to even out dynamics
- Brickwall limit to prevent clipping

## What This Is NOT

- Not zero rhythm (that's Relax or Meditate)
- Not busy or energetic (that's regular music)
- Not nature-first (that's Relax)
- Not sparse gongs (that's Meditate)
- Not guitar or strings (removed per user feedback)
